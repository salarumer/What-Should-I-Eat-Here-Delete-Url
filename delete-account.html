How to Delete Your Account

1
Open the What Should I Eat Here app on your device
2
Tap the menu icon (☰) in the top left corner
3
Select "Delete Account" from the menu
4
Confirm the deletion when prompted
⚠️ This action is permanent
Once you delete your account, this action cannot be undone. All your data will be permanently removed.

What Gets Deleted

  • Your account and login credentials
  • All visit history and restaurant records
  • Personal preferences and settings
  • All associated personal data
